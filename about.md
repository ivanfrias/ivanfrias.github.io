### Who Am I

My name is Ivan. I'm a software developer based in Portugal. I am a simple person with a good taste for life. I usually enjoy relaxing while drinking a glass of wine.
I have had a passion for music since childhood, which drove me to learn an instrument. Heavily influenced by the hard & heavy acts of the 80s, I continue down the guitar learning path to this day! Every now and then you can also find me at an arena enjoying a great live show.

Gaming on Xbox, catching shows on Netflix, reading a great book, or attending stand-up comedy—I enjoy good downtime. Technology in general is a lifelong passion, both from the perspective of an enthusiastic user and as someone who designs, builds, and ships software.

### What Am I Doing

I am currently working at **[8x8](https://www.8x8.com)**, following a brief chapter contributing at **Talkdesk**. 

Over the course of my career—from solution engineering and cloud communications to full-stack distributed systems—my role has evolved into that of an **AI-Fluent Software Engineer**. In this capacity, I actively leverage modern artificial intelligence, LLM tooling, and agentic workflows alongside rigorous engineering principles and automated testing to accelerate software delivery, architect resilient microservices, and solve complex product challenges faster and more effectively than ever before.

### What Have I Done

I started my journey building business applications in .NET. In 2010, I joined Altice (formerly PT Inovação), which gave me strong technical resilience working on telecom systems with demanding schedules, operational SLAs, and legacy architectures.

By late 2013, I joined CarMediaLab, a German startup based in Bruchsal developing hardware and software solutions for the automotive industry. There, I built front-end and back-end applications connecting to vehicle hardware using Java and Vaadin.

In 2017, I joined Fuze Inc. to work on enterprise cloud unified communications, building carrier integrations, number portability microservices, serverless tooling, and automated provisioning systems—leading into 8x8's acquisition of Fuze.
